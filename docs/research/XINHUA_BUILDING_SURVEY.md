## Time and assembly decisions

- Use the existing major residential forms as the working base. Missing exact completion dates must not block a recognizable geographic draft or cause high-rises to be replaced with imaginary old-style houses.
- Keep individual construction-period claims separate. Current portals mix 1990s and 2000s entries, sometimes even on one page; a site-wide construction year must not switch an entire estate on/off.
- Preserve shell identity across time. Repainting, balcony enclosure, signs, detached street shops and enclosure changes are separate appearance or occupancy changes. Recent renovation is not demolition.
- Three street-edge relationships need distinct placement: dwelling behind a fence; independent low building in front/outside a compound; verified commercial space within a larger building. A listing's word “shop” does not establish physical attachment or ownership.
- Reuse a verified building family within matching groups while preserving footprint length, orientation, height class and street setback. Avoid window-by-window clutter, European rooflines and decorative hutong facades. Warm paper texture can carry surface richness.
- Overview region, street segment and entrance retain shared identity. Generated image pixels never set the road or entrance coordinates.
