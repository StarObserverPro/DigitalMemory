# Xinhua source-to-render pipeline

DM-0018, 2026-10-19. The previous failure was that hand-authored compound layouts became the runtime source of truth, while checking only a few tower anchors and road overlap could not catch missing, rotated or invented buildings. R6 removes that dependency.

1. **Acquire bounded data.** The other chat's Sanli snapshot has 249 polygons. The corridor snapshot adds coverage across both sides of Xinhua: 774 source polygons from Overture 2026-09-23.1, including OSM and East Asia building data. Both snapshots contain no usable native heights. Overture records sourced from OSM are not independent corroboration of OSM.
2. **Select and reconcile.** Inspect source polygons over imagery; keep actual slab directions, gaps and articulated tower shapes. The selected plan has 161 masses across eleven groups. Two missing curved Wuli bodies are explicitly traced/generalized. A source segmentation adjacent to Sanli's long western slab is excluded as a separate building. Two unresolved small road-edge bodies are omitted rather than displaced. The school boundary is OSM way 961273731, version 3; the southern neighbouring parcel is excluded.
3. **Estimate Z separately.** Every object records a chosen storey count, range and evidence role. Sanli's front family uses the reported 23-storey class. Bali uses two source outlines and listings reporting 23 storeys. Wuli east has 10/14-storey listing families, with their assignment to the two curved bodies still approximate. Photo/typology estimates fill the rest. Storey-to-metres conversion is illustrative; these are not surveyed heights or an individual-building 20% accuracy certification.
4. **Keep one spatial plan.** WGS84 is converted once by the existing local transform. Original source IDs and outlined geometry remain in `xinhua-spatial-plan.json`; runtime registration is generated from it. Group envelopes organise ground artwork and labels, not building positions. Approximate roofprints retain uncertainty and must not be silently called surveyed footprints.
5. **Compose raster levels.** Use original generated wax/pencil material surfaces on the registered masses. Far: broad masses with tiny low-rise bodies suppressed. Medium: subdued floor rhythm. Near: denser facade marks. All levels share XY, height, bounds and depth ordering. Visible SVG art is prohibited; nested SVG elements only crop packed raster images. Detached shops have their own ground geometry.
6. **Check before promotion.** Runtime compilation checks source/evidence presence, atlas bounds, image/footprint correspondence and clearance against the complete relevant main-road set, including context continuations. Tests cover every mass, inter-building overlap, stable cross-level anchors, the school boundary, the two Bali towers and a deliberately displaced non-landmark building. Actual consumer renders cover desktop far/medium, Sanli/Wuli/school close views and mobile. These are native renders, not browser gesture evidence.

The working time window is the existing approximate 2000-era memory composite. Contemporary imagery can orient surviving buildings but does not prove their construction dates. Earlier eastern low fabric and the detached frontage opposite Sanli follow the user's reconstruction instruction. The data does not silently appear in 1995 or unmapped life stages.

Production regeneration: install Pillow, numpy and Shapely 2.1 in an offline build environment, then run `python scripts/build-xinhua-art.py`, `npm test`, `npm run build`. The application itself remains dependency-free native JavaScript. Keep overlays, QA pictures and source photographs outside the repository; retain concise outcomes in the task packet. Art acceptance remains the user's decision.
