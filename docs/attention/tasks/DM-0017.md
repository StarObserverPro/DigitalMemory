# DM-0017 — Sanli junction open 3D-geography acquisition

## Intent and scope

User asked for actual 3D map data around 新华街三里, not another freely arranged illustrated neighborhood. The bounded area covers the Sanli frontage, including the intersections 新华街×韩庄路 and 新华街×东兴里. This is public geography and MUST NOT encode residential details or private photos.

Actual 3D meshes and panoramic textures visible in commercial Chinese map clients are not thereby openly licensed for extraction. Acquire reusable Overture Maps building and building-part polygons instead, then audit source-provided heights. Overture building geometry may be a satellite roofprint rather than true ground footprint. Missing fields are never guessed.

Current geographic data: WGS84 Sanli parcel `osm-way-897762350` in `content/public/overview-registration.json`; named OSM roads in `content/public/orientation.json`. Existing `content/public/map-context.json` OSM building coverage is sparse at Sanli. The ten-building inventory in `docs/research/XINHUA_BUILDING_SURVEY.md` is a working tower/slab grouping, not matched individual addresses. Main and existing painted overview/street view are protected.

## Acceptance

- Download both Overture `building` and `building_part` for a small explicit WGS84 bbox with official CLI, pin the client, and retain ODbL attribution.
- Keep original source polygons in `content/reference/sanli-open-geometry.geojson`, matched to existing junction-road centerlines, and keep original Overture IDs and nullable height/floor/parent IDs.
- Emit `content/reference/sanli-open-audit.json` with raw/extracted counts, number lying inside Sanli, available source heights, proximity to roadway flags and source hashes.
- Missing data or source geometry incompatible with user photos must **not** be silently replaced by simulated shapes or painted into the runtime.
- Candidate footprint is not a registered historical structure. Building-part height allows a 2.5D approximation, not a licensed photorealistic provider-native 3D model.

## Delivery and evidence

Review branch `agent/dm-0017-sanli-open-geometry`; workflow `.github/workflows/sanli-open-geometry.yml` fetches the small source extract and commits QA outputs to **the same review branch only**, subject to actual CI permissions. Main, merge, deployment, historical imagery and production art are unaffected.

Review must inspect workflow conclusion and count/floor coverage from the real source dataset. If there are significantly fewer than ten source polygons within the estate or height data are missing, mark the dataset as incomplete and compare to licensed satellite imagery/photos before considering construction.

No further task is automatically activated and no future painting or integration is approved by this acquisition alone.
