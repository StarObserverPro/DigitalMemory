## School identity is not site identity

Keep the institution separate from a geographic site and from the institution's occupancy of that site. School moves must not relocate old memories.

The [historical-site conclusions](research/FENGTAI_PRIMARY_OLD_SITE.md) retain the former primary-school address and broad relative location. Both historical school boundaries and entrances remain unresolved. Current campuses do not substitute for those identities.

Resolve the historical institution/site and the supported street-facing entrance or uncertain range before activating a school attachment. Exact rear campus extent is only needed if the selected representation uses it; it is not a prerequisite for a gate/note. Current school-name search is insufficient, and no exact address is invented.

## Interaction emphasis

Build around walking pace, repeated street-side views, familiar turns, small shops, and school entrances. A physically small memory point may receive more detail and prominence than a large background compound. Background blocks can remain planar or generic volumes.

**Explicitly excluded:** the long extension to the 丰益桥 high school, continuous cycling interaction, the unrelated newer junior campus, the broad World Park/railway-hospital periphery, and a full-town reconstruction. Keep future cycling, transit, and jump-route modes representable in the data model without implementing them now.

## Route-first scope and locality

The overview's normal entry targets are the registered streets/routes of this compact network. Gates, buildings and remembered positions are attached inside them; not every geographic place becomes an overview shortcut. Resolve route side, approach and institution/site identity before activation, while leaving unknown rear geometry sparse.

The hospitals and other complex institutions discussed as interaction examples are not new construction areas. Their note/sequence/local-scene patterns generalize the architecture without activating the previously excluded railway-hospital periphery. Likewise, names of additional schools are conceptual examples until explicitly included and registered.

Initial validation should use one bounded Xinhua route with a gate/note, a synthetic image sequence and a small local-scene return path. Include one editable wall/railing/frontage segment and a synthetic same-site sequence with uncertain dates. This is a suggested future acceptance fixture, not authorization to construct it now.

Calendar windows remain approximate. No complete historical satellite base is established by the selected data.
