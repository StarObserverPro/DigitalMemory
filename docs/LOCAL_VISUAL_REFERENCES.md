## Drawing decisions

- North-side Xinhua sample uses short plain apartment flats with independent ground-floor bays; south-side sample uses modest low service walls and a separate plain gate. These are typology proxies, not surveyed occupants.
- No decorative courtyard eaves, continuous veranda, fantasy landmark, household detail, vehicle fleet or clutter is added. Paper texture carries most surface variation.
- A 12 m apartment module has two roughly 4 m frontage slots; a 10 m low service segment has one 5 m slot. Entrances attach to a slot and an exact route parameter.
- The theatre has an abstract venue vignette east of the ring road. Its exterior has not been researched sufficiently for a likeness claim. Its underlying geographic point is unchanged; its drawing-space offset is explicit.
- Contemporary road source geometry remains contemporary orientation data. It is not silently assigned to a childhood year. Sparse periods with no approved occupant remain unknown.
- Anonymous child sprite is a reusable 1.4 m profile, independently configurable from date selection. No personal chronology or residential address is encoded.

## Source exclusions

An image-search result initially labeled as a useful apartment reference resolved to **安宁里（北区）, 海淀**. It was rejected as outside the project area. Search result captions alone are not accepted as location evidence. Generic renovation articles about 长辛店 were likewise excluded from Xinhua-specific drawing decisions.
