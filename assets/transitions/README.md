# Street-entry journal presentation

Revision: journal-doodles-r1 (paired art); the accepted original hand is retained. This is the user's 2026-10-28 replacement direction after rejecting the first procedural hand/copy and a realistic sketch: a Haring-inspired, rounded, continuous black-outline writing hand with flat fill. The user accepted the composed animation, including revised pacing and motion, on 2026-10-28.

- `hand-haring-r1.png`: built-in ImageGen output, 1536×1024 RGBA, three matching writing poses. The consumer crops the original pixels, excludes adjacent-frame bleed and registers each pen tip. No private reference image or source photograph was used. Rejected generations are not included.
- `street-journal-r1.woff2`: a renamed, glyph-subset derivative of [Long Cang](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/longcang), containing all 227 distinct revised text/date characters. The original font and subset are licensed under the included SIL OFL 1.1; copyright belongs to the Long Cang Project Authors. The original font is not bundled in addition to this subset.
- `manifest.json`: pinned delivery hashes and provenance/review status. `scripts/build.mjs` verifies them before packaging.

The 38 source-numbered dated fragments are user-reviewed fictional decorative observations requested for the transition. They are not autobiographical memory records and never enter the memory catalog; a decorative reference to 看丹桥 does not register or verify that landmark. They replace all initial map/drawing-instruction phrases.

The brief used for the selected hand: Keith Haring's bold rounded continuous black contours and flat warm-white fill; a clear right-hand pencil grip; three close animation poses with aligned pencil contact points; true transparency; no skin rendering, hatching, cuffs, gradients, labels or background. The original generic page block used rounded curves, a bold black outline and a flat paper fill; the paired doodles now replace that block. Continuous path timing, small local hand/doodle pose variation, runtime frame registration and the user-requested short wrist fade belong to `src/explorer/entry-transition.js`. The rejected procedural long arm has been removed; the original raster bytes remain unchanged.

## Selected paired doodles

30 individual 512×512 RGBA PNGs in doodles-r1 were selected for product use after grouped built-in ImageGen generation and lossless cell cropping. Corrected cells replace an oil-stick/flat-cake confusion and a missing ruler; unused cells and grouped/trial sheets are excluded from Git. Source copy numbers and mappings are in src/explorer/journal-catalog.js. Its drawing polylines derive from a downsampled black-ink mask of each selected PNG (thresholded RGB/alpha, skeletonized, simplified and ordered); they are presentation metadata only. The runtime reveals original raster pixels along these paths and finishes the incomplete wash. Steam/wind/other accents are procedural; they are not a separate generated sprite sheet.

The shared ImageGen brief specifies isolated bold rounded Haring-inspired black contours, very few geometric lines, light imperfect pen/pencil texture and one incomplete pale color swipe per motif, transparent background, no labels or frame. The selected subjects cover breakfast, weather, bus/bicycle, street animals/food/keys and childhood school/play objects. Original hand pixels, its three registered poses, wrist fade, manuscript paper and pause/reading contracts remain separate from the doodle assets. The generic rounded block is superseded by this paired-art consumer. The existing SIL OFL license also covers the regenerated renamed font subset; no source TTF or new runtime dependency is bundled.
